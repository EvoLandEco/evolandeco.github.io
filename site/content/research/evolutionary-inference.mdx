## Learning from phylogenetic trees

Phylogenetic trees contain information about the processes that produced biological diversity. My work combines neural inference with stochastic diversification models to study those processes.

For EvoNN, I designed ensemble neural networks that learn from tree structure, branching times and summary statistics. Alongside this inference work, I developed models and fast simulators for diversification influenced by evolutionary relatedness.

## Models, inference and software

The work connects biological questions with simulation, model fitting and evaluation. EvoNN provides neural inference from phylogenies; evesim supports simulation of evolutionary relatedness dependent birth–death processes. The modelling and software work connects diversification assumptions to simulations used for inference.
