## Performance

The production preview uses the staged public assets at a 1466 × 832 viewport with device pixel ratio 2. Chromium uses the Apple M4 Max through Metal. The measured load reaches ready in 2.02 seconds. A 119-frame drag sample has a median interval of 10 ms, a 95th percentile of 11.8 ms and no interval above 50 ms.

Three samples of three seconds each give median main-thread busy time of 26.8% during rotation, 9.05% in fullscreen Trends, 7.75% in Reports and 9.88% in One Health. Offscreen and reduced-motion samples issue no WebGL draws or SVG attribute writes. These measurements describe the browser main thread, not total CPU or GPU utilization. They do not predict iPhone performance.

Node accessibility attributes follow visibility changes. This avoids 36,720 redundant attribute writes per three-second rotation sample, with the same drawing count. The measured rotating main-thread share is 26.8%, compared with 28.75% when these attributes are written each frame.

With fourfold CPU throttling, the largest report renders all 816 figures and 99 claims in 2.12 seconds. Switching to Trends and back takes 1.54 seconds. Its JavaScript heap after garbage collection is 82.1 MiB; this excludes process and GPU memory. Scope dialogs mount on demand.
